User Service: Responsible for handling user registration, authentication, and profile management. (DONE)

Team Service: Manages football teams, their players, and formations.

Card Service: Handles the collection and trading of digital football cards.

Auction Service: Allows users to auction their digital football cards and bid on cards from other users.

Match Service: Handles the scheduling and management of virtual football matches.

Score Service: Calculates and updates the scores of players and teams based on their performance in virtual matches.

Wallet Service: Manages users' virtual currency (e.g., Sorare's Ethereum-based cryptocurrency) and transactions.

Marketplace Service: Provides a platform for buying and selling digital football cards.

Ranking Service: Ranks users and teams based on their performance in virtual matches and card collections.

Notification Service: Sends notifications to users for various events like auction updates, match results, etc.

Analytics Service: Collects and analyzes user data to provide insights into user behavior and preferences.

Payment Service: Handles payment processing for purchases of virtual cards, currency, etc.

Authentication Service: Centralized service for handling user authentication and generating access tokens.

Image Service: Manages the storage and retrieval of images, such as player avatars and card designs.

Admin Service: Provides administrative functionality to manage users, content, and configurations.
